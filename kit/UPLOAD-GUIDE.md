## 6. Настройки проекта
**Название:** Vesper — An Evening in Riga

**Описание:**
> Vesper is a concept website for a fictional evening restaurant in Riga's Old Town.
>
> Instead of a brochure, the site is one evening told through scroll: the street, the door, the room, the table — until the signature dish lifts off the photograph and becomes the menu, and the night ends with choosing a table. The camera is the visitor; native scroll is never hijacked.
>
> Art direction, UX/UI, web design and motion — Marta Jakovleva.
